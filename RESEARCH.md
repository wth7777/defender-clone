# Research Summary: Ultima IV-VI Mechanics

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Exploration**: Tile-based overworld (grass, forest, mountain, water) with random encounters.
2. **Towns**: Visit towns for healing, buying equipment, training, gathering rumors, and quests.
3. **Dungeons**: First-person wireframe (Ultima VI) or top-down (IV/V) exploration with puzzles, traps, monsters, and treasures.
4. **Quests**: Given by NPCs or discovered via exploration; often involve retrieving items, defeating monsters, or making moral choices.
5. **Character Progression**: Increase stats (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, etc.) via training at guilds using gold earned from combat and quests.
6. **Combat**: Turn-based (Ultima IV/V) or real-time with pause (VI). Player commands party members to attack, cast spells, use items, flee, etc.
7. **Magic System**: Learn spells from trainers or find scrolls; each spell requires reagents (IV/V) or mana (VI). Spells have combat and utility uses.
8. **Virtue System (Ultima IV)**: Eight virtues (Honesty, Compassion, Valor, Justice, Sacrifice, Honor, Spirituality, Humility). Player's actions affect virtue scores; achieving Avatar status requires maxing virtues.
9. **Day/Night Cycle**: Affects NPC schedules, shop hours, and certain encounters.
10. **Food/Hunger**: Party consumes food while traveling; running out leads to damage over time.
11. **Saving**: Can save at any time (usually via menu) to disk; in browser, localStorage.
12. **Party System**: Recruit companions with distinct personalities and abilities; manage their equipment and stats.
13. **Conversation**: Keyword-based typing (IV/V) or menu-driven (VI). Ask about name, job, health, etc., to get clues and rumors.

## Green Lantern Lore Essentials
- **Central Power Battery on Oa**: Source of green light (willpower) for all Green Lantern rings.
- **Green Lantern Ring**: Powered by willpower; allows wearer to create hard-light constructs limited only by imagination and willpower.
- **Oath**: "In brightest day, in blackest night, no evil shall escape my sight. Let those who worship evil's beware, my power—Green Lantern's light!"
- **Emotional Spectrum**: 
  - Green: Willpower
  - Yellow: Fear (Sinestro Corps)
  - Red: Rage (Red Lanterns)
  - Orange: Greed (Agent Orange)
  - Blue: Hope (Blue Lanterns)
  - Indigo: Compassion (Indigo Tribe)
  - Violet: Love (Star Sapphires)
  - Black: Death (Black Lanterns)
  - White: Life (White Lantern Corps)
- **Guardians of the Universe**: Immortal beings who created the Corps and guide it from Oa.
- **Corps Structure**: Sector-based; each Lantern patrols a sector of space.
- **Major Enemies**: Sinestro (former GL, leader of Sinestro Corps), Atrocitus (Red Lantern leader), Larfleeze (Orange Lantern), Black Hand (Black Lanterns), etc.
- **Key Locations**: 
  - Oa: Corps headquarters
  - Earth: Sector 2814 (home of Hal Jordan, John Stewart, etc.)
  - Korugar: Sinestro's homeworld (oppressive regime)
  - Zamaron: Star Sapphire homeworld
  - Ysmault: Red Lantern homeworld
  - Qward: Antimatter universe, weapon forgers
  - The Bleed: Interdimensional space

## Fusion Concept
- Replace Ultima's eight virtues with the seven colors of the emotional spectrum (plus White/Life maybe). Player's actions affect alignment with each color, influencing ring power and story outcomes.
- The ring's constructs are the "magic" system; willpower is the mana/resource.
- Towns become alien outposts, space stations, or alien cities.
- Dungeons are alien ruins, enemy bases, or hazardous environments.
- Combat uses ring constructs (shield, sword, grenade, cage, etc.) and can be turn-based for mobile.
- Quests involve investigating emotional imbalances, resolving conflicts between spectrum factions, and upholding the Corps' duty.
- Day/Night cycle could represent local star cycles or be replaced by "sector threat level".
- Food/Hunger could be replaced by "willpower depletion" needing recharging at Power Batteries or via willpower-restoring actions.
- Party system could be fellow Lanterns or alien allies recruited.
- Conversation uses menu-driven choices (like Ultima VI) with topics based on Green Lantern lore (inquire about sector threats, ring status, emotional disturbances, etc.).
